# Surface brief: Catálogo Qi Buxo (src/App.tsx)

Mode: Operate (the visitor picks products and sends an order). Code-led. Brand world pinned by the client's existing artwork, so no concept roll was run (a brief-pinned world beats the roll).

Audience/job: client on a phone, usually arriving from Instagram/WhatsApp, comparing sizes and prices, assembling an order for a party or the day. Action: send the order to the shop's WhatsApp. Content: real products, sizes and prices from 40 flyers; photos cropped from the flyers; no invented delivery, hours or testimonials.

Unresolved: WhatsApp number, Instagram, city (src/config.ts); original product photos; Kit PP discount wording; G size slices for Torta de Ninho assumed 20.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The catalogue is the shop's own flyer set turned into one working order screen: every size and price visible without a tap, and the order leaves as a ready-made WhatsApp message. Refuses the generic "elegant patisserie" page (serif, pastel, hero photo) and the generic delivery-app grid.

OWN-WORLD: Chocolate-brown (#47301c) fields against warm cream (#fbefe4); strawberry red (#b5302a, the mascot's scarf) reserved for prices and the add action. Hand-cut heavy lettering (Londrina Solid, caps, bege outline echoing the flyers) for titles and prices; Nunito for reading. Heart as the only ornament. Kits are solid chocolate blocks; products are cream cards with a photo, size chips that carry their own price, and a red add pill. Dashed notice box with heart pills mirrors the flyers' "Informações" panel. Recognizable with content removed by the brown/cream split, outlined lettering and the mascot badge.

STORY: Visitor sees it is Qi Buxo's catalogue in one glance, finds a category by chip or search, reads price per size on the card, adds items, reviews them in a sheet and sends the order via WhatsApp. They believe the prices are real because every figure comes from the shop's own flyers.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Mobile 375: cream top bar (logo + wordmark left, brown "Pedido" pill right); compact chocolate band with outlined "CATÁLOGO" title, one-line promise, mascot badge on a cream tile at right, search field; sticky category chips immediately below; the first section's heading and first kit visible at the bottom edge. Primary action (Adicionar / Pedido) is always reachable at the right thumb. Desktop: same order, 3-column grid under a wider band.

FORM: Standard e-commerce-style catalogue shell (sticky header, scrollspy chips, sectioned grid, bottom sheet / side drawer cart); world enters through palette, lettering, kit blocks and the dashed notice only. Pinned by brand, no concept-seed run, seed key: none.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
